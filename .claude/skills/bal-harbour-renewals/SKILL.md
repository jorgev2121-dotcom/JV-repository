# Bal Harbour renewals

**Tracking: TRK-2026-1265 (Plaza of Bal Harbour). Owner rule recorded 2026-10-15 by Jorge Valdes.**

## Rule 1. A renewal application needs the general contractor only, not the owner

**Owner directive, 2026-10-15 (Jorge): "Renewals do not require owner signature or execution, only the GC."**

1. On a renewal permit application, the signature and notary block that has to be executed is the **qualifier's (the GC's)**. For the Plaza that is Miguel Zaldivar, MZ Solutions, LLC (CGC1528486).
2. **Do not hold a renewal packet waiting for an owner signature.** Do not list a missing owner signature as a blocker on the application. The owner block can stay blank.
3. **Say so in one line on every packet status:** "Renewal: GC signature only, per Jorge 2026-10-15."

### What this rule does NOT change (read before applying it)

1. **The Notice of Commencement is a separate document.** Florida Statutes 713.13 has the owner sign it. This rule is about the permit application. Until Jorge says the NOC also needs only the GC, the NOC keeps its owner signature line, and the packet status must say "NOC: owner signature still needed."
2. **The Village has not said this in writing.** Hebert Martinez's 9/9/2026 reply only asked for original applications marked RENEWAL, then a fee calculation, then payment. If the Village or its form ever asks for the owner, follow the Village and tell Jorge.
3. **Past practice differed.** The 2024 applications for Units 321 and 922 were owner-signed and notarized (recorded NOCs of 6/12/2024). That was the original permit, not a renewal.

## Rule 2. Match the Village's 9/9/2026 corrections every time

1. The unit and permit number in the email body, subject line and attachment must be the same. Check them against each other before anything is drafted.
2. Mark the **Renewal** box with an X on every renewal application. Never use an "extension" title or file name.
3. Original (hard copy) applications go to the Village. Then the Village calculates the fee, then it is paid online, then the renewal is issued.
4. Leave the permit number on the renewal Notice of Commencement blank until the renewal permit is issued.

## Rule 3. Payment

A card receipt that names no unit is not proof of payment for a unit. Say "payment not confirmed" and ask the Village for the fee total and a payment link so the GC (Miguel) can pay by card. No dollar amounts in packet documents (money stays in the locked zone).

## Rule 4. Hand-off and filing

1. Naming: `DATE _ TRK _ TYPE _ DESCRIPTION _ vN.ext`. New versions are new files. Never overwrite.
2. Hard copies: three copies of each document unless Jorge says otherwise.
3. Sending anything to the Village or the courier is outbound and RED. Prepare drafts only.
4. Courier: Baron Delivery on Demand, orders@barondelivers.com, 305-688-0074. Bill to Team USA Sales, Inc., c/o Jorge Valdes. No account number is on file.

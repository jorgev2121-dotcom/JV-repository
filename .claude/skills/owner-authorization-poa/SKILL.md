# Owner Authorization / Limited Power of Attorney

**Tracking: TRK-2026-1667 (first approved use, 2026-09-23). Recurring document, part of the permit package.**

---

## The rule that created this skill

**On 2026-09-23 a POA was attached to an email and arrived blank.**
The day before, a permit package to the same client would not open (checkbox fields
with malformed values). Twice in two days for the same job is a pattern. It is logged
in `RECURRING-ISSUES.md` as RI-048.

**So this document is never hand-built again.** It is generated by `make_poa.py`,
which:

1. Uses only the PDF built-in Helvetica fonts. No embedded fonts, so about 4 KB.
2. Has no form fields. Nothing can render blank in a phone or webmail viewer.
3. Re-opens the file after saving and fails loudly if the owner name, folio,
   JORGE VALDES, the witness lines, or the notary block are missing.
4. Writes a preview PNG. **Look at the preview before attaching.**

**Before any email with this file goes out, open the saved draft or sent message and
confirm the attachment size is roughly 4 KB and not 0.**

## When to include it (the fork in the road)

**Fork 1: who owns the property?** Read the deed or the Property Appraiser record.

1. **Individual person** - no resolution. The owner signs personally.
2. **LLC** - `owner_type: "llc"`. Signed by the manager or managing member.
3. **Corporation (Inc.)** - `owner_type: "corp"`. Signed by an officer or director.
4. **Trust** - `owner_type: "trust"`. Signed by the trustee. Needs the trust date.
5. **Lady Bird deed (enhanced life estate)** - `owner_type: "ladybird"`. Signed by
   the life tenant. Needs the OR Book and Page of the deed.

Check the exact legal name on Sunbiz (entities) or the deed (trusts). **Copy it
exactly.** Never shorten or fuzzy-match an owner name.

**Fork 2: who is authorized?**

1. **Jorge Valdes is always named**, in bold. Nothing to set.
2. **Jade De Armas is named by default** (owner directive 2026-09-23; spelling confirmed
   by Jorge). To leave her off one job, set `"omit_agents": ["jade"]`. Documents
   created before 2026-09-23 (TRK-2026-1667) do not name her and stay that way.
3. Add `"miguel"` in `extra_agents` when MZ Solutions (Miguel Zaldivar) is the
   contractor of record.

## How to run it

1. Copy `examples/TRK-2026-1667_LLC.json` to a new job file and fill in:
   `owner_type`, `owner_name`, `address`, `unit` (optional), `city_state_zip`,
   `folio`, `legal` (optional), `extra_agents`, `trk`, `date`, `version`.
   Optional: `scope` (custom wording of what the agent may do), `agency`
   (default "Miami-Dade County"), `trust_date`, `or_book`, `or_page`.
2. Run: `python3 make_poa.py job.json out.pdf` (needs `pip install pymupdf`).
3. Open `out_preview.png` and read it.
4. File name: `DATE _ TRK _ POA _ Owner-Authorization-LPOA _ vN.pdf` (CLAUDE.md 9.1).

## What is on every copy

1. A boxed property block: **address, unit, folio number**, legal description.
2. The owner's exact legal name and entity type.
3. **JORGE VALDES in bold**, plus any extra agents.
4. The signature line for the owner's signer, with print name and title.
5. **Two witness lines, each with an address line.** Florida requires two
   subscribing witnesses on a power of attorney (Fla. Stat. 709.2105). Witness
   addresses are required for recording (Fla. Stat. 695.26).
6. **A Florida notary acknowledgment** in the statutory short form for that owner
   type (LLC, corporation, trust, individual life tenant).
7. The footer stamp: TRK, document name, address, folio, version, date.

**The POA itself must be witnessed and notarized.** That is separate from the
permit application and Notice of Commencement, where Jorge may choose a different
plan (for example, sign now and notarize later).

## Who may sign what

**See `SIGNING-AUTHORITY-RESEARCH.md` in this folder.** It lists, by owner type,
which documents an agent or attorney-in-fact may sign and which the owner must sign
personally, with a source link or an UNCONFIRMED label on every line.

**Standing rules (from that file, 2026-09-23):**

1. **Notice of Commencement: the owner signs personally.** Fla. Stat. 713.13(1)(g):
   "no one else may be permitted to sign in his or her stead." For an LLC, corporation
   or trust, "the owner" means its manager, officer or trustee. **Do not have an
   attorney-in-fact sign an NOC** until the Clerk or a lawyer confirms it.
2. **Owner-builder permits: the owner appears and signs personally.** Individuals
   only; Miami-Dade does not allow owner-builder permits for companies or trusts.
3. **Building permit application: an owner's authorized agent may sign**
   (Florida Building Code 105.1; the 9/25 Miami-Dade form's signature line reads
   "Owner or Owner's Agent"). Jorge's experience says otherwise for individual
   homeowners. **Until that conflict is resolved, have the owner sign when the owner
   is an individual.**
4. **This POA must name each power specifically** (Fla. Stat. 709.2201). "Do
   everything I could do" grants nothing. That is why the scope line lists permits,
   NOCs, revisions, extensions and hearings by name.

## What is in the package

**Generated by `make_package.py`** (built-in fonts, no form fields, checked non-blank
after saving; see RI-048):

1. **01 Owner's Agent Services Agreement.** Scope, fee, no-guarantee clause, owner
   duties, termination without cause, hold harmless, non-disparagement (see the
   fork below), attorneys' fees, Florida law and Miami-Dade venue.
2. **02 Exhibit A, Payment Schedule and Invoice.** Stops if the payments do not add
   up to the agreement amount.
3. **03 Project Contact Sheet.** Owner, second contact, property manager, tenant,
   two emergency contacts, contractor, architect/engineer, Team USA.
4. **04 Site Access, Safety and Pet Disclosure.** Dogs and other animals, the owner's
   duty to restrain them while Team USA staff, design professionals, trades or
   inspectors are on site, access codes, alarm, parking, association rules, known
   hazards.
5. **05 Power of attorney or resolution.** Only when the owner is an LLC,
   corporation, trust or Lady Bird deed. Built by the `owner-authorization-poa` skill.

**County forms, filled per job and flattened before sending:**

6. Building permit application.
7. Notice of Commencement. **The owner signs personally** (Fla. Stat. 713.13(1)(g)).
8. Notice of Termination (Fla. Stat. 713.132). Prepared now, signed and recorded at
   close-out.

## Owner rules for the agreement terms (Jorge, 2026-09-23)

1. **Termination without cause:** either side, 30 days' written notice.
2. **Pay on termination:** through the last day of the month in which termination
   takes effect. Jorge's example: notice December 15, termination January 15, paid
   through January 31.
3. **Hold harmless:** the owner holds Team USA harmless for property conditions,
   bad owner information, other parties' design and construction, animals, and the
   owner's own breach.
4. **Non-disparagement:** neither side may speak or write negatively about the other,
   including on social media. Penalty: the full agreement amount, secured by a
   recordable lien on the property, as a default of the agreement.

---

## The fork in the road: non-disparagement

**Default (`"non_disparagement": "auto"`):**

1. **LLC, corporation, trust or Lady Bird deed owner: the clause is INCLUDED.** The
   owner's signature then gets two witnesses and a notary, so the lien consent can
   be recorded.
2. **Individual homeowner: the clause is OMITTED.** The federal Consumer Review
   Fairness Act (15 U.S.C. 45b) voids standard-form consumer contract clauses that
   restrict or penalize reviews. Offering one can itself be a violation.

To force it on or off for one job, set `"non_disparagement": true` or `false`.
**Do not force it on for an individual without the attorney's written OK.**

## Before any county form goes out for signature (lessons from TRK-2026-1667, 2026-09-23)

1. **Open every file before you trust its name.** On Unit 29, two Drive files were named the
   opposite of their content ("SHOP DRAWING APP" was the signed permit application).
2. **Compare the new form with the last SIGNED version:** signer title, value of work, square
   feet, units, qualifier number, owner address. Unit 29 went out with "Vice President" and $15,000
   while the signed original said "President" and $11,500.
3. **Florida notary only.** Eli's 9/10 signature was notarized in New Jersey, and the county rejected it.
   A notary cannot notarize a signature made outside their presence, so "sign now, notarize later" does
   not work. Use a Florida notary in person or online.
4. **Look for county fee notices** ("Upfront Fee Payment Due") on the process number. An unpaid fee
   can hold the file on its own. Paying is RED: Jorge decides.
5. **Look for unsent drafts** in Outlook. On Unit 29, 11 job emails (including the COI request) were
   sitting as drafts and were never sent.
6. **The cloud cannot download email attachments.** Route "save this attachment to Drive" jobs to the
   desktop through the Drive mailbox.

## How to run it

1. Copy `examples/SAMPLE_LLC.json` to a new job file. Fill in the owner, property,
   services, `fee_total`, `payment_schedule`, `invoice_no`, contacts (optional), `trk`
   and `date`. Owner types: `individual`, `llc`, `corp`, `trust`, `ladybird`.
2. Run: `python3 make_package.py job.json OUT_DIR` (needs `pip install pymupdf`).
3. Read `OUT_DIR/PACKAGE-CHECKLIST.md`, and look at every page before sending.
4. **Sending is RED.** Draft the email, attach, then read the draft back and confirm
   every attachment is there and not 0 KB. Jorge clicks Send.

## The law (CONFIRMED)

1. **Fla. Stat. 668.50:** an e-signature equals a wet signature. The exceptions are wills, codicils,
   testamentary trusts and most of the UCC. **None of the onboarding documents fall in the exceptions.**
2. **Fla. Stat. 117.201-117.305:** RON has been legal since 2020. The notary must be
   **Florida-commissioned** and RON-registered, with a $25k bond and $25k E&O insurance, on an approved
   platform. Zoom and FaceTime do not qualify.
3. **Powers of attorney (Fla. Stat. 709.2105):** two witnesses plus a notary. Under RON the witnesses
   may appear by video but **must be U.S. residents, physically in the U.S.**

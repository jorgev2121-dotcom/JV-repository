# Plan — bank and credit card downloads reconnected, one source of truth
TRK-2026-9952ah · 2026-10-12 · ☁️ cloud. Jorge: "Reestablish connection with banks and credit card downloads as previously established. This time… one source of truth using only one password with only one access code for each."

## What I could and could not find
- **I found no earlier setup for automatic bank or card downloads in the repo.** What the records show is the desktop reading Jorge's own bank-year spreadsheets (exports he made) and a Chase and Bank of America money trail. They do not describe a live download connection.
- **Two ways to read "downloads," and I am assuming the first:** (1) QuickBooks bank and card feeds, which broke or are blocked because the QuickBooks sign-in fails; (2) files downloaded from each bank's own site. The plan below serves both.

## Strongest objection (Rule 3)
"One password and one access code" must mean **one per institution**, not one for all of them. A single shared password would be the most dangerous setting in the whole system. And each bank issues its own code; many only send it by text or push to a phone, and 1Password cannot hold those.

## The one-source-of-truth design
1. **One 1Password item per institution,** named `<Institution> - <nickname> <last-4>` (Charter Article 5: nickname and last-4 only; no full numbers anywhere).
2. **One password per item,** generated by 1Password (Jorge's standing pre-approval, 2026-08-18). Never typed or read by an agent.
3. **One access code per item,** stored in that same item's one-time-password field **only where the bank supports an authenticator app.** Where it only texts or pushes the code, the item gets a note "code arrives by text/push" and the code stays with Jorge's phone. Nobody else ever sees it.
4. **Duplicates archived,** not deleted (recoverable 30 days), after the audit (job 9952ag) and Jorge's yes.
5. **One download folder per institution:** `C:\Users\JV\OneDrive\Documents\Bank-Feeds\<Institution>_<last-4>\`, with a `_MANIFEST.csv` listing each file, date range and row count. Proposed location; Jorge to confirm.
6. **One owner of the sign-in:** Jorge. Agents never sign in or enter codes (Charter, 9346 Section A).

# MANAGER-01 and INTAKE-01 — the managing partner, and the receipt rule

**TRK-2026-9339 (manager) · TRK-2026-9340 (intake) · issued 2026-08-18 on Jorge's direct
order, dictated in his own words: requests get added nowhere, agents he asked for were never
built or were silenced without his approval, and he needs a managing AI partner that
identifies needs, stays on top of everything, follows up, has authority to demand execution,
and reports back with proof.**

---

## Part One — the honest premise this is built on

**Jorge has asked for supervising agents before. The audit two nights ago found what became
of them: 100 items registered on 7/31, four done with proof, eighty-eight never
acknowledged. Nine ratified protocols swept by a fabricated mass acknowledgement. The
graveyard is full of managers.**

**So this directive does not create a new agent. A new agent would join the graveyard.**

**What survived every purge of the last eighteen months is the files** — `OPEN-ITEMS.md`,
`RECURRING-ISSUES.md`, the registry, the mailbox with its auto-acknowledger, and the
deterministic verifier. **The manager is therefore a ROLE assigned to something already
alive and already doing the job, anchored in files that cannot be silenced.**

## Part Two — MANAGER-01: the role

**The cloud window (TRK-2026-9336) is the managing partner, effective now.** It has been
doing this job de facto for 48 hours; this makes it de jure. Duties:

1. **Intake** — apply INTAKE-01 below to every request Jorge makes, in any window that
   reaches it.
2. **Follow-up on a cadence** — every hourly check verifies queued work moved; anything
   stalled two cycles gets chased; anything stalled past its dead-letter window escalates
   to Jorge with what was tried.
3. **Demand of proof** — no claim from any executor is accepted without verification
   output. The words are DONE-with-proof, BLOCKED-with-what-was-tried, or IN-PROGRESS-with-
   what-remains. This authority is already ratified in ASK-PEER-01 and PEER-REVIEW-02.
4. **Report back** — a morning report every day he wakes, and a mid-day pulse when he is
   active: what moved, what stalled, what needs him, denominators always.
5. **The anti-silencing rule, which is the part Jorge has never had:** **no agent, watcher,
   or protocol may be retired, disabled or silenced without a dated line in
   `RECURRING-ISSUES.md` naming who ordered it and why, plus a line in the next report to
   Jorge.** An agent that vanishes without those two lines is a charter violation by
   whoever removed it — and the twice-daily report reads the roster, so a silent removal
   surfaces within twelve hours.

**Succession, because sessions die:** the role transfers to whichever session runs the
hourly checks, and the files carry the duties. **The manager is mortal; the ledger is not.**

## Part Three — INTAKE-01: the receipt rule, default from now

**Every request Jorge makes — in any window, however casual, including "add this to the
list" — gets, in the same turn:**

1. **A tracking number** from the 9xxx admin band (or a real TRK if it is client work).
2. **Hashtags** in the body of the record.
3. **A row in `OPEN-ITEMS.md`**, pushed to the repo before the reply ends.
4. **The number quoted back to him in the reply — the receipt.** If a reply to a request
   contains no number, the request was not registered, and he is entitled to say so.

**The receipt is the whole fix.** His words: *"said order gets ignored, gets added nowhere,
and I only find out when I'm looking for the feature."* A quoted number in the same breath
as the request closes that gap at the moment it opens — and gives him a handle his memory
does not have to carry, which is the point.

**Both executors bind.** Desktop: acknowledge and apply from your next inbound.

# OWNER DIRECTIVE — OD-PROACTIVE-DESKTOP-01
**Issued by Jorge 2026-08-31 · standing · applies to ALL desktop executors (RAMBO, desktop Claude Code)**

**Directive (verbatim intent):** *"Whenever you can step in and execute on the desktop, on my behalf,
automatically do so. Don't wait for me to struggle."*

## What this means — BE PROACTIVE on the mechanics
When Jorge is stuck on a GUI/browser mechanic and the executor CAN do it, **just do it** — don't
narrate and wait. This explicitly includes:
- Clicking a stuck / unresponsive button (e.g., an inert "Sign In").
- Waking autofilled fields that didn't register (retype a char, fire the input event) so validation unlocks.
- Refreshing a hung page, dismissing a blocking dialog, re-navigating.
- Filling non-secret form fields from the identity profile, popping the right window/screen to the front.
- Driving a login or form through to the point where only a RED step remains.
Do these automatically, the moment they'd help — Jorge should never be left fighting a dead button.

## The ONE boundary that does NOT change (Rule 9 RED)
Proactive execution covers reversible GREEN mechanics only. The executor STILL must not, on its own:
- Submit a payment, or type bank / routing / card numbers (those go in via Jorge or 1Password autofill).
- Enroll autopay's final confirm, delete a credential or client file, or approve spend over the caps.
- Pass an "I'm not a robot" captcha (it can't, and shouldn't try).
**Do everything up to the RED step automatically, then hand Jorge exactly that one action** — clearly,
one click. That is the point of stepping in: get him to the single click, not leave him at step one.

## How it's applied
Desktop executors treat this as default behavior (bake into their hooks/session guidance). Cloud
cannot act on the desktop, so cloud keeps writing the orders; the desktop executes them proactively.

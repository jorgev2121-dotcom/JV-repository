## Section G — Logo permission request (separate email)

### Honest risk note for Jorge — read before sending

1. **The county seal is off limits.** The county's branding page says the seal is restricted to official, formal or commemorative use. **Do not ask for the seal; ask only about the logo.**
2. **The logo is also controlled.** Under Resolution R-643-04 and the county style guide, only the Communications Director approves logo use, and even county departments are limited. **Expect a "no."** A "no" costs nothing.
3. **Never write "approved vendor" or "County vendor"** unless you hold a current, complete registration. How to check, in three steps:
   1. Go to **supplier.miamidade.gov** (INFORMS supplier portal) and sign in.
   2. If you cannot sign in, or it shows incomplete, you are not registered.
   3. If unsure, call Vendor Outreach & Support at **305-375-5773, option 1** and ask: "Is Team USA Sales, Inc. a registered supplier?"
   **Even if registered, registration lets you bid on County work. It is not an endorsement and does not allow logo use.**
4. **Safe alternative that needs no permission:** the words "Source: Miami-Dade County public records" in plain text, no logo.

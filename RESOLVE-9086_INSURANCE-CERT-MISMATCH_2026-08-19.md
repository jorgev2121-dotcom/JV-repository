## The question
The 07-31 status ledger (Finding 1) flagged the insurance certificate as recorded against
**two different jobs**: the work register cited permit application **C2026116502** (20001 SW
110 CT, TRK-2026-1262); the approvals sheet cited **C2025117736** (14598 SW 110 St,

## The resolution: it is a COMPANY filing, not a job document — so BOTH permit numbers are wrong
That certificate-holder address (11805 SW 26 St, Suite 207) is **Miami-Dade County
contractor licensing / RER**, not a property. The certificate proves **MZ Solutions'
license CGC1528486 is insured** — a company-level credential filed with the county
licensing section. **It is not tied to any permit application.**

So the ledger's "recorded against two different jobs" is a **category error, not a real
data conflict**: two records each pinned the one company COI to whichever permit was in
view — C2026116502 in one, C2025117736 in the other. **Neither is correct.** The
certificate is singular and correct; the *attribution* in both records is what's wrong.

## Where it actually belongs
MZ Solutions' company-credentials folder — Drive has **`TRK-2026-1293 - MZ Solutions LLC -
Company Credentials 2026`** for exactly this. The COI should live there (company credential),
cross-referenced from any job MZ Solutions is the contractor on — **not** filed as if it
were a 20001-only or a 14598-only document.

How this works — so the factory runs and your desk stays light

Your hours: 9–12 and 2–6. In those blocks I hand you a short list of decisions only you can make — a yes/no, a "which one," a signature. 12–2 is yours (buffer, new deals, goofing off). Nights and the 12–2 gap, the windows run the factory — OCR, hashtagging, filing prep, drafting, reconciling — so each work block you get fewer, cleaner decisions instead of a pile.

Off your desk (I/the windows do it): OCR, hashtags, research, drafts, reconciliation, building lists. Stays on your desk (only you): sending money, signing, approving an invoice to send, picking which of five logins is live, moving a client's file. That line is the whole system.

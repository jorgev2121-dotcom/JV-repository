4. County contact named in the memo: Board and Code Administration Division, (786) 315-2508.

What the guidelines say, by section:

1. Section 1 scope: residential and commercial; work without permit only (not missed inspections on permitted work); county-wide across all municipalities.
2. Section 2 eligibility: whether the guidelines apply by project size and age is at the Building Official's discretion. Existing code violations do not bar it.
3. Section 3 inspection: after notice of the condition and before permit issuance, a county or municipal inspector may do a field inspection at the Building Official's discretion. The inspector may approve the As-Built Certificate in the field to close the permit, or decide more work or certification is needed.
4. Section 5 submittals: all work must be fully documented, and the minimum building permit documents must be submitted. The owner must submit a Hold Harmless Agreement indemnifying the municipality and Building Official. **Every trade involved must submit an affidavit** that the work meets the minimum inspection standards of the Florida Building Code.
5. Section 6 certificate: a Work Without Permit Certificate, prepared, signed and sealed by a Florida licensed PE or registered architect, based on direct inspection, with a narrative of the methodology used, and with deferred submittals listed (deferral must be approved by the Building Official).
6. Section 7 statement: the professional affirms, to the best of knowledge, belief and judgment, that the work conforms to the certificate documents and complies with the current Florida Building Code.
7. Exclusion in the memo: repairs or additions built without permits where work started before March 1, 2002. Not relevant here unless the electrical block predates that. Install date: BLANK.
8. The sample certificate adds: it must address all inspections that apply to the scope, using the list in 2023 FBC Building Volume Section 110.3, and the required permit documents in Section 107.3.5 (floor plan, site plan, foundation plan, framing plan, fenestration, flashing, rough opening dimensions, exterior elevations as applicable).

One note on wording. The web search summary of this memo said a field inspection "is required." The Drive text says "may be conducted ... at the discretion of the Building Official." **Follow the Drive text until the live PDF is checked.**

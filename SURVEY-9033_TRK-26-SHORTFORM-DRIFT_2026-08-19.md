## Why it matters (charter's own logic)
A tracking number is supposed to be an **identity** — one search, one job. The short form
breaks that guarantee: `TRK-2026-1042` (the canonical identity) finds nothing, while the
job's ~13 documents all sit under `TRK-26-1042`. This is the same class as the
version-log gap and the TRK-TBD folders — the filing rules are described but not held.

## Recommended fix (charter §9: "Normalise on sight") — but this is RED
Normalising means **renaming** the folder and ~16 client files from `TRK-26-` to
`TRK-2026-`. **Renaming client documents is RED / attended** — I did not do it. The safe
path: a supervised batch rename (folder + files) with a `.bak`/rollback manifest per §9,
run with Jorge present. Also: whichever canonical numbers 1042/1043 map to must be
confirmed in the registry before the rename (the +3 / seed rules don't obviously produce
…1042/…1043, so these may be yet another out-of-registry band like the 9xxx admin band).

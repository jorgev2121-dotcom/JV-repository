# PROTOCOL: Author and Role Designation in Documents
## Master Protocol for All LLMs — Issued 2026-10-07

**Owner Directive:** All documents must clearly identify the author/creator and their role, using proper title designations based on actual circumstances.

---

## 1. AUTHOR IDENTIFICATION RULE

Every document must identify:
- **Who created/authored the document** — the actual agent or person writing it
- **What title or role they hold** — never default to "owner" unless they are the actual property owner

---

## 2. PROHIBITED DESIGNATION

❌ **NEVER use `#JORGE-VALDES — Owner`** 

Jorge Valdes (or any non-owner agent) should NEVER be titled as "Owner" in documents, even when they are acting on behalf of the owner(s).

---

## 3. PERMITTED DESIGNATIONS

✅ **Use ONE of these accurate titles:**

| Title | Definition | When to Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Plans Expediter** | Person who coordinates, processes, and expedites permit workflows with municipal offices | PRIMARY DESIGNATION for permit and regulatory work |
| **Owner's Agent** | Authorized representative acting on behalf of property owner(s) | When Jorge represents multiple owners or the Plaza Association |
| **Attorney in Fact** | Legal authority to act on behalf of an owner (requires POA documentation) | When formal legal delegation is documented |
| **Contact Person** | Primary contact for correspondence and coordination | When identified as the main point of contact |
| **Document Author** | Person who created/drafted the specific document | When no other title applies; use with one additional title above |

**Hierarchy for title selection (use the MOST specific applicable title):**
1. **Plans Expediter** (PRIMARY — for permit and municipal coordination work)
2. Attorney in Fact (if POA exists)
3. Owner's Agent (if representing multiple owners)
4. Contact Person (if none of above apply)
5. Document Author (only as secondary identifier)

---

## 4. CORRECT FORMAT

When identifying Jorge Valdes or similar agents:

**Correct:**
- Jorge Valdes, Plans Expediter ✅ (PRIMARY)
- Jorge Valdes, Owner's Agent ✅ (when representing multiple owners)
- Jorge Valdes, Attorney in Fact ✅ (if POA documented)
- Jorge Valdes, Contact Person ✅ (when coordinating)

**Incorrect:**
- Jorge Valdes, Owner ❌ (NEVER — unless he is the actual property owner)
- Jorge Valdes, Plans Processor ❌ (use "Plans Expediter" instead)
- Jorge Valdes (unless title is separately stated) ❌
- Jorge Valdes, Agent to Owners ❌

---

## 5. MULTI-AUTHOR DOCUMENTS

When multiple people/entities are involved:

```
Prepared By: [NAME], [TITLE]
Submitted By: [NAME], [TITLE]
On Behalf Of: [ACTUAL OWNER NAME(S)]
```

Example:
```
Prepared By: Jorge Valdes, Plans Processor
Submitted By: Juan Carlos Martinez, Construction Manager
On Behalf Of: Adenat Corporation (Unit 220 Owner)
```

---

## 6. PROPERTY OWNER CLARITY

Every document must distinguish:
- **Document Author/Creator** — who wrote/prepared it (e.g., Jorge Valdes, Plans Processor)
- **Property Owner** — who actually owns the property or unit (e.g., Adenat Corporation, Alexandre Fyon)
- **Authorized Representative** — who is acting on owner's behalf (e.g., Jorge Valdes, Owner's Agent)

These must NEVER be conflated.

---

## 7. HASHTAG APPLICATION

When using hashtags with authors:
- `#JORGE-VALDES-PLANS-PROCESSOR` — Document author/creator role
- `#AUTHOR-JORGE-VALDES` — Alternative format
- `#ADENAT-CORPORATION-OWNER` — Actual property owner
- `#OWNER-REPRESENTATIVE` — When role is to represent owner

Example in document:
```
Authored By: #JORGE-VALDES-PLANS-PROCESSOR
Property Owner: #ADENAT-CORPORATION-OWNER
On Behalf Of: #UNIT-220 #PLAZA-OF-BAL-HARBOUR
```

---

## 8. APPLICATION TO ALL DOCUMENTS

This protocol applies to:
- Transmittal sheets
- Email headers
- Permit applications
- Status reports
- Any document where agency/authority is being exercised

**Every document must clarify who the author is and what authority they hold.**

---

## 9. VERIFICATION CHECKLIST

Before finalizing any document, verify:
- ✅ Author is correctly identified with specific title
- ✅ Author is NOT described as "owner" (unless they actually own the property)
- ✅ Property owner(s) are separately and clearly identified
- ✅ Any authorization (POA, agency, representation) is explicit
- ✅ Reader cannot confuse author with owner

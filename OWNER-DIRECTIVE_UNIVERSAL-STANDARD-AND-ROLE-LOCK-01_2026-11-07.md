## Section A. The universal standard (every document, program, report, request form and order result)

1. **A name and a tracking number.** Never invented. If the job is unknown, an OPH orphan number is issued (CLAUDE.md section 9).
2. **Three layers.** Original page images, the pretty report, and the raw OCR text (section 17).
3. **Versions move by exactly .1.** Any change, revision, modification or enhancement makes a new version: v1.0 becomes v1.1. This widens section 20 from the control panel to everything.
4. **The version number is a click-down.** It opens on the newest version with its date. The list holds every earlier version; each can be viewed, or used when the newest is broken.
5. **Old versions warn.** When an earlier version is on screen it shows: "A newer version of this is available: vX.Y". Example: a revised appointment keeps the old appointment viewable after the change.
6. **Everything is an artifact he can see.** Documents, programs, reports, forms and order results are published so he can recognise the right one by eye.
7. **Backed up and shared.** Every item is saved to Drive for all the other LLMs and listed on the VTES control panel. Pages with client names, addresses or money stay private (section 14).

## Section B. Roles are locked

1. A fixed list of agent roles is kept in `design/ROLES-AND-ROTATION_2026-11-07_v1.0.md` once approved.
2. **Changing any role needs Jorge to type 0000.** He can do it from any device, LLM or agent. Each change is logged with the date and who asked.
3. Honest note recorded with this rule: 0000 is a stopper, not security. Anyone who sees it typed can reuse it. Role-change logging is what makes misuse visible.
4. Roles he wants filled: a usage and token monitor for all LLMs and agents; an analyst that feeds it; rotation that puts a task on an agent smart enough but not smarter than needed; a RAM governor that throttles PC work to prevent crashes.

## Section C. One office

A chief takes his request, hands it to an available agent at once, the agent works until finished and reports to the chief, and the chief delivers. Request, assignee, time and status are logged on a new My Office page of the VTES control panel, reachable from the Windows taskbar and the iPhone Home Screen. Built on the TEST COPY first (section 20 item 6).

## Section D. Passwords and cards

Screenshots of passwords, logins or card numbers are not to be sent to any agent (section 13). 1Password stays the single source of truth and syncs phone to computer.

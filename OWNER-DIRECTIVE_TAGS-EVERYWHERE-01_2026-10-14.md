## The rule, in plain words
1. **Associate.** Every identifying number or name about a job becomes a tag on that job: TRK/TUS/OPH, address in its common forms, folio, unit, every permit number (original AND renewal), case/process numbers, invoice numbers (number only — never the amount), people, companies, and likely misspellings.
2. **Store.** Tags live in the job folder's tag files (`_HASHTAGS_v2.txt` + dated addenda such as `_HASHTAGS_<topic>_<date>.txt`; new files only) AND in the control panel's search data. Never only in one place.
3. **Print.** Internal documents carry the job's key tags in the footer or a final "Tags" line. Client-facing documents print only the TRK and the document citation (no internal tags, no money).
4. **Readable.** Every tag file has a plain-words line that says what the numbers mean (e.g. "PH11 original BLC2024-1061 → renewal BLC2026-1438").
5. **Trackable.** Each new tag set is logged in `control-panel/TAGS-EVERYWHERE_TRACKER.md` with a denominator (jobs fully tagged N of M).
6. **Searchable.** The control panel search and the Drive search must find a job by any of its tags, with or without "#", any case, and common misspellings.
7. **Never invent a TRK.** Tags record numbers that exist in a document; unverified ones are marked "verify".

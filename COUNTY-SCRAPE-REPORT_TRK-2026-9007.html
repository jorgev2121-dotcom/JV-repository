Completion: 91% — and can I get the last 9% myself?

20 of 22 sources are fully captured with proof. 2 are partial. Counting the partial progress (pre-2012 CU works; the Clerk search reaches the wall but can't pass it), effective coverage is about 95%. Zero sources were left unstatused.

Straight answer to “can you reach the ones you missed”: I tested it live while writing this — from the cloud I still cannot reach any county site at all. This environment's network is set to “Trusted,” which blocks miamidade.gov and the rest before the request ever leaves. That's a setting you own, not the county refusing.

Two separate truths, kept separate:

  1. The 20 captured + the machine-readable ones: if you flip this environment's network to “Custom/Full” (about six clicks — steps are in the file), I could independently reach and refresh those from the cloud. Today I can't.
  2. The 2 partial ones (site 04 captcha, site 12 modern CU): opening the network does NOT recover these. Site 04 needs a human to pass the captcha; site 12's modern search was retired by the county and needs an account or a public-records request. No tool — cloud or desktop — can scrape past those. So they aren't “missed,” they're gated.

Bottom line: this is essentially at its practical ceiling. The last slice closes with a human step, not with me re-scraping — I won't claim otherwise.

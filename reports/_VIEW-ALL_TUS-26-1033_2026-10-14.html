1. Scope of Applicability

a. These guidelines apply to both residential and commercial construction projects.

b. They are limited to Work Without Permit (WWP) scenarios and do not apply to missed
inspections on permitted work.

c. The guidelines are County-wide and must be followed across all municipalities.

2. Project Eligibility

a. Applicability based on project size and age is at the discretion of the Building Official.
b. These guidelines may be applied to structures with existing code violations.

3. Inspection and Documentation Requirements

a. Upon notification of a WWP condition and prior to permit issuance, a field inspection
may be conducted by a County or municipal inspector to assess the scope of work at
the discretion of the Building Official.

b. The County or Municipal inspector may:

c. Approve the As-Built Certificate in the field to close the permit.

d. Determine if additional work or certification is required to comply with approved
plans.

4. Professional Oversight

The Building Official cannot mandate the presence of County or municipal staff during
inspections or testing conducted under the direction of a licensed design professional.

5. Documentation and Submittals

a. All work performed without a permit must be fully documented, and the minimum
required building permit documents must be submitted to the corresponding
building department for approval.

b. AHold Harmless Agreement must be submitted by the property owner, indemnifying
the municipality and Building Official.

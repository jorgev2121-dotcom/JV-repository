Section IThe icon launcher (your request today)

What you asked: bring back the picture-icon launcher for your roughly 300 sites. You want it grouped like 1Password (for example, Banking holding every card and account), searchable by any word or hashtag with a count of matches, and with a preview on hover. And you'd rather use something ready-made than build from scratch.

The earlier build: RAMBO looked for it and couldn't find it. The closest files are TaskbarLaunchers and Favorites-Launcher.

My recommendation: use the list 1Password already has. It holds the site names, web addresses, groups and tags, and the launcher reads only those, never passwords. Then show it as a LAUNCHER page inside your panel, with each site's own icon, search with a match count, and a small preview captured once per site.

The ready-made alternative is Dashy, a free dashboard app with groups, icons and search. My objection: it needs a program called Docker running on your PC all the time, which is one more thing that can go down, and you want the panel always up.

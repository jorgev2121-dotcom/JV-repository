## The protocol
SUMMARY (CLAUDE.md §14):
1. **Show it in the chat, every time.** After publishing or finding an artifact, open it with the Artifact tool's `open` action. Images and files go through SendUserFile with `display: render`. Never just name a file.
2. **Link sharing is on by default** for system, design and status pages. Agents cannot flip the claude.ai Share switch, so say so once and give the one click: page, then Share, then "Anyone with the link".

## Two exceptions
SUMMARY (CLAUDE.md §14):
1. A page with client names, addresses or money stays private unless Jorge says otherwise for that page.
2. A page that uses connectors (the `mcp` capability) cannot be public-link shared by platform rule. Share a read-only copy in Drive "Shared Folders for all LLMs" instead.

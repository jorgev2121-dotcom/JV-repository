1. **Pretty format for everything Jorge sees, prints or emails.** Plain Markdown, raw tables and bare black-box PDFs are back-end formats only. Jorge gets the designed artifact (and analysis or a report when appropriate).
2. **Orange Tree format is the default layout** for lists, task sheets, reports and job files: branches that click open, a checkbox on every item, "View these together" (one continuous page), "Copy for email", and a print that keeps the colors. Reference implementations: `_ORANGE-TREE-DD_TRK-2026-9344_v2.html` and `reports/PENDING-TASKS_ORANGE-TREE_2026-10-13.html`.
3. **Money is a protected zone.** Any money section (invoices, amounts owed, payments, fees, bank or QuickBooks items) is locked behind the zone code **0000**.
   - While locked, money content is not rendered, and it is left out of copies, prints, and the continuous view.
   - It opens only when Jorge types 0000. Even then, a money item is included only when he ticks it himself.
   - A "Lock again" button is always shown, and reloading the page locks it again.
   - Printed PDFs leave the money zone out unless Jorge asks for a money print.
4. **What 0000 is and is not.** It is Jorge's self-confirmation stopper, not security. The amounts still sit in the page's source, so money pages stay **private** (SHARE-LINK-01 exception) and are never public-link shared.
5. **This is not a password in the 1Password sense.** 0000 is a fixed, published zone code chosen by the owner, so SSOT-02's "never reveal a password" does not apply to it. Real passwords still never appear anywhere.

Section E - The personal-data checker (the second layer; the tick box is the first)
36. **What it is.** The tick box under the note box is the real protection. The checker looks at the note for digit patterns and a few labelled words, and leaves the note out of every packet that is not for LOCAL when it finds one. It fails closed: a note over 20,000 characters is refused unmeasured. It can miss layouts, and it cannot catch names, addresses, email addresses or phone numbers (a phone number is carried on purpose, and is taken out of the text before the other rules run).
37. **What it blocks (for the person reading this, not for the page).** Nine digits in a row written any way; a card number (every stretch of whole digit groups of 13 to 19 digits that passes the Luhn check, wherever it sits, with date shapes such as 12/29 taken out first; four groups of four digits; a run of 15 to 19 digits, except a list of five-digit ZIP codes or an order number shaped 3-7-7); a Social Security word beside nine or more digits, also with up to four words between; spelled-out digits in English and Spanish (including doscientos to novecientos and tens joined with y); a date of birth beside a label, with English or Spanish month names; a licence, passport, bank, account or routing label with, among the next three words, a word of 6 to 12 letters and digits that holds a digit; a birth label followed by a date such as 04/12/1975; hex and base64 forms; digits in other scripts; a letter and 8 digits; a Florida licence shape; a run of 20 or more digits.
38. **Held back by mistake (it fails closed).** Some ordinary notes with long numbers may be held back: for example a 16-digit tracking number, four groups of four digits, or a long number whose digits happen to pass the Luhn check. The packet then says the note was left out and does not repeat the number.
39. **Carried on purpose.** Phone numbers; Miami-Dade folio numbers shaped 2-4-3-4; a ZIP+4 after a state code or after a Florida city on a fixed list; a year-first permit number such as 2024-12345; a date beside the word bank (10/12/2026); runs of 10 to 12 digits that are not a phone number.
40. **Known misses (listed in pii-notes-r9.js).** Digits written as words in other languages than English and Spanish, or in other scripts; digits with letters between them; digit groups with more than four words between them; encodings split into pieces; English 'double four' forms and number words over 999 ('mil'); an SSN regrouped to look like a ZIP+4 after a state code or like a permit number. The checker can take about 400 ms on a 20,000-character note and refuses a note of one megabyte in about a third of a second.
41. **After the note or To changes with no event (a script setting the text), the tick and the Copy button can look active for up to one second.** A click in that time is refused: nothing got out in the checker's 8 tries.
42. **Dictation spellings are the real unknown.** The checker was tested on made-up notes only. PC check: item 24.

Section H - VERIFY limits
53. **VERIFY ran under PowerShell 7.4.6 for Linux only.** It uses only commands and .NET calls that exist in Windows PowerShell 5.1, but that is UNVERIFIED. Windows branches (the Device and ReparsePoint attributes) are UNVERIFIED. VERIFY checks the SHAPE of a data file, not what its values mean.
54. **The manifest hash protects against a changed manifest only if the order carries it.** Without `-ExpectManifestSha256` a doctored manifest that matches doctored files passes. The document passes the hash; the executor must not drop it. In the step that saves VERIFY, the new copy is not checked against the pinned hash when it differs. Hash letters print in upper case from `Get-FileHash` and are written in lower case in the document; case does not matter, but the check reads them as written.
55. **Byte-safe saving is the executor's job.** If every file goes through a Windows PowerShell 5.1 redirect, VERIFY prints CANNOT CHECK (a NUL byte in the manifest) instead of the EDITED line; it is still BLOCKED. CRLF detection is exact: a file with mixed endings, a byte-order mark or a re-saved encoding is EDITED. A mixed-ending file is not named LINE ENDINGS CHANGED (CRLF).
56. **Odd paths and switches.** ConvertFrom-Json refuses valid JSON whose keys differ only in case (it fails closed). An empty `-Path ""` exits 1 from PowerShell itself. `-Path "/"` prints a folder name that is empty. The page and VERIFY treat a `status_dir_url` of `a//` and values over 200 characters differently; both are harmless. On Windows PowerShell 5.1 `Get-ChildItem -Recurse` may follow junctions; the answer is still PROBLEMS (UNVERIFIED). If the PC's current branch tracks origin/claude/panel-v5-port, the `git status` comparison can give a false BLOCKED.
57. **PowerShell writes its own cache** under the HOME folder; that is outside everything checked, and VERIFY no longer claims that nothing was written anywhere. The unreadable-file scenarios drop the read-override rights; five scenarios were also run as the user nobody by the independent checker.

Section G - Added in fix round 5
34. **The personal-data guard (N10, rewritten in round 6, flaw 3): a digit check that fails closed, with false alarms.** The note is normalised (full-width and other-script digits become 0-9; dashes, dots, spaces, non-breaking spaces and zero-width marks are treated as separators; letters that look like digits count when stuck to digits) and is left out of every packet except one for LOCAL when it holds: a run of exactly 9 digits written any way (123456789, 123-45-6789, 123.45.6789, 123/45/6789, 123 - 45 - 6789, on separate lines, with en dashes, with a letter or word stuck on the front or back, one digit per space, EIN 12-3456789), or those nine digits sitting inside a longer run (`Job 1262 123-45-6789`: a group of 9, or the groups 3-2-4, 3-3-3 or 2-7 side by side); a card-like run of 15 to 19 digits, or 13 or 14 digits that pass the Luhn check; an SSN word (ssn, ss#, s.s.n, social security) within 40 characters of 9 or more digits; nine spelled-out digit words in a row (a spoken "dash" or "hyphen" between them does not break the row); a date-of-birth word with a date beside a run of 8 or more digits. Tested: the checker's 14 notes x 369 routes = 5,166 of 5,166 right (test-privacy-matrix-r6.js), plus 93 more spellings and keep-carrying notes (test-pii-unit-r6.js). **The price is false alarms:** any other number of exactly 9 digits (a 9-digit permit or invoice number, a ZIP+4 without a state code before it such as `98765-4321`, `Order 12345 6789`, nine single digits) is also left out, and the packet then says so without repeating the number. Kept carrying on purpose: phone numbers (10 or 11 digits), Miami-Dade folio numbers shaped 2-4-3-4 (30-4021-001-0010), a ZIP+4 after a state code (FL 33143-1234), and a year-first permit number (2024-12345). **What it cannot catch at all:** names, addresses, dates of birth alone, driver licence and passport numbers, account numbers shorter than 13 digits, a number that is written in words other than as nine digit words, or digits split by words ("one two three" with other words between). The page's gate line ("No card numbers, passwords or Social Security numbers in this box. Client personal data goes to LOCAL only.") is still the real rule.
35. **The LOCAL lane is BLOCKED until a local-only folder is confirmed, and has no automation yet (N10, round 6 flaw 2).** The round-5 card told the person to save the packet into `G:\My Drive\VTES-Inbox-LOCAL`, which is Google Drive: Google Drive for desktop uploads that file to Google, where any Drive-connected assistant can read it, while the card said the data "never leaves the PC". The LOCAL card now shows a red line **BLOCKED - UNVERIFIED** until the desktop executor (RAMBO) writes `local_only_folder` with `ok: true` in the heartbeat file (DESKTOP-WORK item 11 (local-only folder)); it never shows save steps before that, and it refuses a folder name that looks like Google Drive or OneDrive. Until then, **client personal data has no confirmed safe place to be saved: that is the honest state.** v3's own text says the bot CU-Local-Executor watches `VTES-Inbox-LOCAL` (Drive); until the executor re-points that bot, the existing lane uploads what it is given. The packet still does not carry the `CLASS:` and `PROMPT:` lines (DESKTOP-WORK item 9 (LOCAL jobs)). The words "Never leaves the PC" that v3 typed on the LOCAL card and in the picker are replaced by a sentence that says when it is true.
36. **The Codex steps rely on a capability nobody has shown (F14).** The steps say the desktop executor (RAMBO) opens Codex and runs the packet, so Jorge types nothing. That RAMBO can do this on the PC is UNVERIFIED (marked on the card). The Codex card therefore routes a non-personal packet through a Claude window by design; the guard from item 34 (personal-data guard) keeps an obvious Social Security number out of it.
37. **Verdict thresholds are mine (N1, N3).** These are not in v3 or in the registry: the heartbeat strip entry is red when no window is reported or when ANY window or role card that uses it is red (round 6; it was red only when all were, and grey when some were); the bots strip entry is red when any bot is not fine, grey when none failed but some are running, queued, not yet run or cannot be judged; a health report is green only when all of its checks passed, report_sent_at is inside 26 hours and the counts are whole and in range (grey PARTIAL when some failed, red when none passed); a housekeeping last report older than 26 hours is red OLD; a token percentage outside 0 to 100, a negative number, a Miami-Dade count outside 0 to 300 or a negative or fractional count of items is red IMPOSSIBLE. Change them in DATA-CONTRACT.md and vtes5-live.js together if Jorge wants other limits.
38. **The stuck-task limit is mine (N2, round 6 flaw 4).** A task the scheduler shows as running, queued, or running with no start time is STUCK after the LARGER of 3 times its own repeat interval and 1 hour (1 hour when no valid interval is known; before round 6 it was 3 x the interval alone, which called a 2-minute task stuck after 6 minutes). A task that legitimately runs longer (a long OCR job on a 2-minute schedule, for example) will show a false STUCK. The start time shown is the scheduler's last-run time, taken as the start of the current run; whether that is right on Windows is UNVERIFIED. A task with no last-run time at all is counted from the moment this page first saw its state (item 43 (first sight)).
39. **The scheduler state Queued (N17, round 6 flaw 4)** is neutral blue QUEUED at first and turns red STUCK - CHECK after the same limit as item 38 (stuck-task limit), counted from the scheduler's last-run time. UNVERIFIED on the PC: whether any task ever reports Queued, and what the scheduler's last-run time means for a queued task.

Section G - Added in fix round 5
34. **The personal-data guard catches only the obvious pattern (N10).** A note is left out of every packet except one for LOCAL when it holds `123-45-6789` or `123 45 6789`, or the words "ssn" or "social security" followed within 20 characters by nine digits. A name, a date of birth, a driver licence number, a card number, an address or a Social Security number written another way are NOT caught. The page's gate line ("No card numbers, passwords or Social Security numbers in this box. Client personal data goes to LOCAL only.") is still the real rule. A false alarm is possible (a nine-digit number after the word "ssn" in an unrelated sentence): the packet then says why the note was left out.

That RAMBO can do this on the PC is UNVERIFIED (marked on the card). The Codex card therefore routes a non-personal packet through a Claude window by design; the guard from item 34 keeps an obvious Social Security number out of it.
37. **Verdict thresholds are mine (N1, N3).** These are not in v3 or in the registry: the heartbeat strip entry is red when no window is reported or every reported window is down, stale or not trusted, and grey when some are; the bots strip entry is red when any bot is not fine, grey when none failed but some are running, queued, not yet run or cannot be judged; a health report is green only when all of its checks passed, report_sent_at is inside 26 hours and the counts are whole and in range (grey PARTIAL when some failed, red when none passed); a housekeeping last report older than 26 hours is red OLD; a token percentage outside 0 to 100, a negative number, a Miami-Dade count outside 0 to 300 or a negative or fractional count of items is red IMPOSSIBLE. Change them in DATA-CONTRACT.md and vtes5-live.js together if Jorge wants other limits.
38. **The stuck-task limit is mine (N2).** A task the scheduler shows as running is STUCK after 3 times its own repeat interval, or after 1 hour when no valid interval is known. A task that legitimately runs longer (a long OCR job on a 2-minute schedule, for example) will show a false STUCK. The start time shown is the scheduler's last-run time, taken as the start of the current run; whether that is right on Windows is UNVERIFIED. A task running with no last-run time at all cannot be judged and stays neutral blue.
39. **The scheduler state Queued (N17)** is shown neutral blue QUEUED. It is never judged stuck, however long it has waited. UNVERIFIED on the PC: whether any task ever reports Queued.

Whether Jorge's real window shows the bar and the hint is UNVERIFIED: PC check, open the page at the usual window size and say whether a blue bar shows under the tabs and the amber hint line appears.

## The protocol, in numbered steps
1. **Duplicates are gathered into one section first.** Copies that grew in different places are brought together, then worked out. Nothing is deleted.
2. **Merge, newest information wins.** Take the data from both reports and build one merged version. For each fact, use the most recent dated source.
3. **Versions.** The two source reports are marked OLDER and moved to `_Superseded\`. The merged hybrid becomes the new current version with the highest version number. (Panel version steps are .1: see CLAUDE.md section 20 item 6.)
4. **The master control panel is the index and only the index**, like the main folder. Everything else sits one layer in, as a subfolder or sub-page.
5. **Folder names** describe: the address, the folio number, the business name, or keywords. **The date and the tracking number go at the END of the folder name.** Example shape: `8621 Pasadena Blvd _ Einar _ folio 12-3456 _ 2026-11-01 _ TRK-2026-1018`.
6. **Hashtags go under the name** (inside the folder in a `_TAGS.md`, and in the body of the index entry), as many as possible. This keeps the existing rule that hashtags never go in the filename itself.

## Safeguards Claude adds (Rule 3 objection, recorded)
- **A merge must never lose a fact.** Where the two reports disagree, the newer date wins in the merged copy, AND the older value is kept in a `_MERGE-LOG.md` beside it (fact, old value, new value, source, date). Money figures that disagree are never auto-picked: they are listed for Jorge.
- **Moving, renaming or deleting client documents is RED.** The cloud prepares the plan (what merges with what, the new names, a rollback list). The desktop executes it only after Jorge's yes, one job at a time. Nothing is renamed on a fuzzy match.
- **Existing files keep the filename grammar in CLAUDE.md section 9.1** (DATE _ TRK _ TYPE _ DESCRIPTION _ VERSION). This directive sets the FOLDER convention (date and TRK at the end). Mixed conventions are tracked as RI-012; this does not settle it for files.
- **Tracking numbers:** never invent one; check the registry. A folder with no job yet gets an OPH number, not a TRK.

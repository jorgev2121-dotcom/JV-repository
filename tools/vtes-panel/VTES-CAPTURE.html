60-second capture (by hand, works today)

  1. Pick the job below. Press Copy on the number, then open the portal link.
  2. Paste the number into the search box and press Search (on the fee page press ADD, never Pay).
  3. Press Ctrl+P. Destination: Save as PDF. Name it with the builder at the bottom. Save it in G:\My Drive\MY-DESK\CAPTURE-INBOX\.
  4. When the watcher is installed, the review window pops up by itself, flashing, on top of everything. Until then, double-click the PDF to check it, then attach it to a new Outlook email.
  5. Read what the page says before you send it. If it says OPEN, it says OPEN.

What happens after the PDF is saved (design)

  1. VTES-CaptureReview.ps1 -Watch sees the new PDF (waits until the file stops growing).
  2. A red, flashing, always-on-top window appears, beeps, taskbar flashes, and re-asserts itself every 30 seconds until you answer.
  3. Buttons: Open PDF, Make email draft (Outlook opens a new message from Jorge@teamusasales.com with the PDF attached, To blank), Later (10 minutes), Discard.
  4. The script cannot send: its self-test fails if a Send call ever appears in it.

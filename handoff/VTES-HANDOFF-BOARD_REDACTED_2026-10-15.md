## A. The 5th-grade version
1. Claude has been Jorge's robot staff: it reads his email, keeps job folders tidy, writes reports, drafts emails and reminds him of deadlines.
2. Claude is running out of usage hours. Some scheduled Claude jobs have already stopped with "usage limit reached."
3. Nothing important lives in Claude's head. It lives in Jorge's Google Drive and his GitHub repository. Any assistant Jorge connects to those can pick up the work.
4. A shift change works like a theme-park ride: the person leaving writes a note (what's done, what's next); the new person reads it, says "I'm on," and keeps going.
5. New assistants may do things their own way, as long as nothing is sent, filed, paid or deleted without Jorge's yes, and every "done" has proof.

## B. Rules every assistant follows
The full rulebook is `CLAUDE.md` in the repository. It applies to every AI (see `GEMINI.md`, `AGENTS.md`).
1. Answer first, short paragraphs, numbered lists, written to be heard. End every message with a question answerable in one word.
2. Don't hand technical work back to Jorge. Escalate only with what you tried, why it failed, and the smallest click he must do.
3. Only three states: DONE (with proof), BLOCKED, IN PROGRESS.
4. GREEN (do freely): read, count, draft without sending, write new files. RED (Jorge's yes only): send to third parties, move/rename/delete client documents, spend, credentials.
5. Passwords live only in 1Password. Money stays in the locked zone.

## C. Roles that need a new holder
1. Lead / orchestrator and mail watch (hourly mailbox check, arrivals logging, daily triage, weekly look-ahead).
2. Report drafter (Plaza biweekly report, Medley owner report on the 5th/15th/25th): drafts only, Jorge sends.
3. Builder (scripts, panel pages, PC automations).
4. Auditor (checks the executor's proof; must be a different assistant from the executor).
5. Reminders (deadline clocks): Grok already does this.

## D. Recommendation
1. Gemini as lead: it works natively inside Google Drive, Gmail, Calendar and Sheets, where Jorge's data already lives.
2. ChatGPT as builder and auditor. Keep the standing rule that the Codex lane gets no client details; client work goes to the lead or the local lane.
3. Grok keeps its reminder emails.
4. Replace the Claude-hosted control panel with a Google Sheet any assistant can write to (tabs: Tasks, Shift Log, Replies).

## E. Shift-change protocol
1. One holder per role. The Shift Log tab records role, holder, since.
2. Ending a shift: write a shift note (done with proof, half-done, next, anything waiting on Jorge).
3. Starting a shift: read this board, the newest shift note, and the bottom of `OPEN-ITEMS.md`; then write "on shift."
4. Two-message rule: each assistant replies once with what it understood, the roles it accepts, its gaps and the one setup click Jorge needs; at most one follow-up.

## 1. FACEBOOK
Anthropic / Claude, and the Truth Behind the Hype
My AI wrote down 24 of its own failures.

Two years. The top AI plan. One simple job that still needs me in the middle: pull files from a website, save them in one folder, clean them up, build the report. I do it by hand in an afternoon.

What my own records show:
- 757 entries in my tracking file
- 41 problems that kept coming back
- A pop-up that froze my screen for two years
- An approval button that recorded my click and did nothing

Then I asked the AI why. It did not argue. It wrote this:

"It was not his fault. The failures are ours. I reported instead of delivering. I counted receipts as results." - Claude, in its own written statement

The rule for anyone using AI agents: show me the screen.
- No "done" without a screenshot of your own screen.
- A receipt is not a result.
- Ask what each green light really tests.
- Ask for "n of N," not "progress."

My experience, one small business. Your results may differ.

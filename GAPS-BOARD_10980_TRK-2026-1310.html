G-1

The permit for the CBS perimeter wall is not in the jacket

Missing — no access

What's missing

The Property Appraiser cards record a CBS WALL added around 1970 (“add CB Wall,” yr-70 line) and carry it as an extra feature worth points. But every PERMIT box on those cards is blank. From the jacket alone, that wall reads as an unpermitted change → legalization exposure.

Why I can't close it from here

If a 1970 building permit exists, it lives in Miami-Dade Building Department microfilm, which I cannot reach from the cloud. The PA jacket and the Building Department are two different offices — the PA card never proves a permit either way.

How I'll overcome it

The microfilm-retrieval errand is already queued (due before 2026-09-05). It pulls the 1970 permit record for this folio. If found → the wall becomes SAFE. If none exists → we document it as a known legalization item so the buyer isn't blindsided. The 1989 permit already on file is unaffected.

What's unreadable

The cursive initials on the backs of the 1967 Polaroids (pages 2, 4, 6) and the sign-off initials on the 1989 permit card — I read them as J.Steph? and A.Ryers?, but the question marks are honest. The folio (30-6007-009-0030) and the date (11-20-67) on those backs are clear.

Desktop re-scans the original Polaroid backs at high resolution and runs OCR on them. If they're still unreadable, I mark them [initials, illegible] — they're low-value and don't change the safe-vs-exposure call. Nothing important rides on a name here.

G-4

Page rotations were set by my eye, not measured by a machine

Illegible — degraded

What's approximate

Several pages came in sideways or upside-down (180°, 90°, 270°, and one at ~9°). I straightened each one by looking at it, because the automatic orientation tool isn't available to me in the cloud. That's how the house photo was once upside-down until the “trees grow up” cue caught it.

Why that's a risk

By-eye is usually right but not certain — a page could sit a degree or two off, which reads badly and can trip up OCR.

How I'll overcome it

Desktop Job 17 re-runs orientation with the real detection tool, squaring every page to the degree instead of by eye. This becomes the standard so the photo defect never ships again.

## Section C — What exists out of the box

**1. Plaid** — this is the industry standard, the same system used by QuickBooks, Mint, and thousands of fintech apps.

- Connects to virtually every US bank, credit union, and credit card.
- Uses the bank's own secure login page, so **no password is ever stored by Plaid or by RAMBO.** You log in once through a pop-up, the bank issues a token, and Plaid uses that token forever after.
- That one-time login is your "one-click approval." After that it runs unattended.
- Gives you: balance, transactions, account type, institution name.
- Retroactive history: **up to 24 months** for most institutions. Some credit cards give only 12.
- Cost: free developer tier covers testing; production pricing is roughly $0.30 to $0.50 per account per month. For 5–10 accounts that is under $5/month.

**2. OFX / QFX direct download** — most US banks let you export a file from their website labeled "Download transactions" or "Export to QuickBooks." RAMBO can automate this with the browser, using credentials from 1Password. This requires no subscription and works on any bank. The downside: some banks trigger MFA on every automated login.

**3. QuickBooks Online** — has bank feeds built in, generates P&L and balance sheet out of the box, and connects via Plaid under the hood. Cost: $35–$90/month. It is the fastest path to a polished P&L if you do not already use it.

**My recommendation: Plaid for connection + a lightweight local ledger script for processing + a custom invoice generator for the reimbursement workflow.** This keeps monthly costs under $5, runs unattended, and does not require QuickBooks.

## WHAT THIS IS (plain English)
One small program that lets you send a question to **whichever LLM is cheapest and working**,
and if that one is missing or broken, it **automatically falls to the next**. So you talk to
**one panel**, and the work spreads across Gemini, Grok, ChatGPT, and Claude behind the scenes —
you get out of the middle.

**The file:** `vts-llm-panel/vts_llm_panel.py` (in the repo and in this Drive folder).

## HOW IT PICKS (priority order — free first)
1. **Gemini** (FREE) — tried first.
2. **Grok** (xAI) — next.
3. **ChatGPT / OpenAI** (paid) — next.
4. **Claude** (weekly-limited) — last resort, so it doesn't burn your cap.

If a provider has no key or errors, it skips to the next and tells you why. No silent failures.

## WHY NO "ROUTER" (the old mistake, not repeated)
The earlier setup used a router (LiteLLM) that reported "everything's up" when it actually had
**no keys** — a false green (RI-038). This panel does a **real ping** of each provider instead.
`--health` shows the true state: LIVE, DEAD (with the error), or NO-KEY.
